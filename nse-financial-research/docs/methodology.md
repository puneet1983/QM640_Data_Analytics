# Methodology

Use standalone quarterly observations with period starts on or after
2022-04-01 and period ends on or before 2024-12-31. Retain source filenames,
context IDs, tag mappings, and units. Review extraction failures and quality
issues. Resolve duplicate or revised filings explicitly before selecting
one company-quarter observation. Do not assume the latest filing was
available at an earlier research date.

The complete panel requires all 11 calendar quarter ends and nonmissing PAT,
total income, profit before tax, tax expense, and basic EPS. This is a
documented default for data preparation, not a statistical power criterion.
Review currency, unit consistency, sector comparability, symbol changes,
and selection bias before research modeling.
